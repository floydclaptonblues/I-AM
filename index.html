    A note in the margin

    A myth told by an unreliable witness.

    This work imagines Genesis as a compressed story of becoming human. The serpent's claims about fruit, uprightness, death, and burial are speculative fiction, not established paleoanthropology. Human bipedalism developed over millions of years; desire alone does not explain evolution.
